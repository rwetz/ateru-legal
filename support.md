# Ateru Support

Ateru is an independent anime tracker operated by Ryan Wetzstein in Minnesota, United States. For help, privacy requests, security concerns, abuse or rights reports, email [rwetz00@gmail.com](mailto:rwetz00@gmail.com).

For a bug, include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and reproduction steps. Remove private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment information or your complete archive for an ordinary support request. If more information is necessary, we will explain why.

Your archive is stored on your device. Before reinstalling or changing devices, export a backup from Settings → Data. Deleting the app can remove its local library. No catalog account is required. Online search/metadata uses MyAnimeList with Tenrai as a temporary-failure fallback; existing saved library data is available offline. Ateru is independent of both services.

If a widget appears stale, open Ateru to refresh its saved timeline. Include the widget size and Home Screen appearance in a report. Avoid uninstalling as a troubleshooting step before safeguarding your archive.

For privacy requests, describe the information or action you want; do not attach government ID or an archive by default. For content or rights concerns, identify the title, catalog URL/ID, your contact details and the basis for the report. We will review reports and applicable obligations. This contact is not a representation that a statutory DMCA agent has been registered.
