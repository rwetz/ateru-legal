# Ateru Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026.

## Who operates Ateru

Ateru is operated by Ryan Wetzstein personally in Minnesota, United States. Contact [rwetz00@gmail.com](mailto:rwetz00@gmail.com) for privacy questions or requests. This policy covers Ateru's app and our handling of support correspondence. Independent services linked below have their own policies.

Ateru is a personal anime tracker with no Ateru account, developer-operated archive server, advertising, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Local storage does not mean that using online catalog features is anonymous.

## Information and where it goes

| Information | Processing and recipients |
|---|---|
| Series, progress, scores, notes, watch history, collections and preferences | Stored in SQLite and app storage on your device. The developer has no remote access to your archive. Migration keeps local recovery copies of earlier records to protect history. |
| Widget timelines and covers | Shared on-device between Ateru and its widget extension through an App Group. |
| Catalog searches, filters and title identifiers | Sent directly to MyAnimeList, or Tenrai during temporary failures, to retrieve metadata. Identifiers requested during refresh or import can reveal titles in your library. |
| Network information | Catalog and image providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, request path and timing. Ateru does not attach an advertising identifier. |
| Imports, exports and recap images | Imports are processed on-device. Catalog identifiers may be looked up. Exports and images go to a destination only through the sharing action you choose; the receiving service controls its copy. |
| Purchases | Apple processes payment. Ateru checks verified on-device StoreKit entitlements for optional customization and restoration. We do not receive payment-card details or send transactions to an Ateru server. |
| Support messages | If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments through the email service. Please omit private notes and unnecessary personal information. We do not need your complete archive to handle an ordinary support request. |

Ateru does not upload your complete archive, personal progress, scores, notes, local watch-event identifiers or import files to catalog providers. Search text is transmitted as search text, so avoid entering personal or sensitive information there. Ateru has no MyAnimeList login or account synchronization.

## Catalog and other independent services

Current catalog requests use the official [MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/) API and an unofficial [Tenrai](https://tenrai.org/) fallback. The MAL application identifier is sent only to the official API. New cover requests use MAL image servers. Older retained images may contact their original provider's image server until refreshed or cleared. AniList, Kitsu and Jikan APIs are no longer queried. External links open when you choose them.

Read [MyAnimeList's privacy policy](https://myanimelist.net/about/privacy_policy) and [Tenrai's privacy policy](https://tenrai.org/privacy) for their processing. Tenrai states that it operates in Sweden and retains technical request logs for up to 90 days. We do not control these providers' retention or processing. Ateru is independent of these services.

Apple's App Store, device backups, operating-system diagnostics and any reports Apple makes available to developers are governed by Apple's settings and [privacy practices](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Optional customization uses Apple's purchase and offer-code systems; tips are disabled. Opening our externally hosted legal pages or using email also involves those services' ordinary network processing. This policy does not describe those services as having no logs.

## Purposes and legal bases

Information is used to provide the tracking, catalog, import, sharing and purchase functions you request, answer support, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or run targeted advertising. There is no advertising profile or data-broker integration.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies to our processing, the relevant basis depends on the activity: performance of a requested service or contract, legitimate interests in responding to support and securing the app where those interests are not overridden, or legal obligations. We will obtain consent when a particular activity legally requires it. This policy does not treat reading it or continuing to use the app as blanket consent. Ateru does not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

## Retention, deletion and your choices

Local archive data remains until you remove it or its storage is deleted. Settings → Data provides archive export and clearing. Clearing the entire library also removes migration recovery copies. Cached metadata and images may remain separately from the personal archive. Widgets keep a local snapshot that may take time to refresh after changes. Deleting the app may remove its local data; operating-system backups and copies you export can remain elsewhere under your control and the relevant service's settings. Export a backup before changing devices or reinstalling.

We aim to remove ordinary resolved support correspondence within 24 months of resolution unless it is needed for an unresolved issue, legal obligation or legal claim. We minimize what we request and retain. Purchase records held by Apple and provider logs are subject to those services' retention rules.

For data we hold, contact us to request access, correction, deletion or a portable copy, or to object to or restrict processing where applicable. You may also have rights concerning sensitive information, sale/sharing, consent withdrawal and nondiscrimination, depending on your location. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. No sale or advertising-sharing activity currently requires an opt-out toggle. We will assess any future change before introducing it.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify a request; please do not send government identification or a full archive unless a justified, secure process has been agreed. We cannot retrieve data that exists only on your device. We respond within applicable deadlines, including generally one month for covered GDPR requests and 45 days for covered California requests, with notice of any permitted extension. If we decline a request, we explain any applicable appeal route; you can email an appeal to the same address and complain to your competent privacy regulator or state attorney general. These rights are subject to applicable legal exceptions.

## Age, location and security

Ateru is intended for people aged 16 or older. People below the age of legal majority need a parent or guardian's permission where required. We do not collect birth dates or verify age, and Ateru is not intended as a service directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us so we can assess and address it under applicable law. An age statement does not replace any legally required parental consent.

The operator is in the United States. Independent providers may process requests in other countries. Applicable international-transfer and representation requirements must be met where required; this policy does not promise a transfer mechanism or representative that has not been established.

We use safeguards appropriate to the app's design, including bounded import parsing, local database handling and verified purchase entitlements. No software, backup or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and copies you share. Contact us with suspected security issues without including passwords or unnecessary personal data.
