# Ateru Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

Ateru is a personal anime tracker. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK. We do not operate a server that receives your watch archive.

## Your library is yours

Your library stays on your device. Ateru stores your series, progress, scores, notes, watch history, collections and preferences locally. There is no Ateru account or server-side copy of your archive. It also caches catalog details and artwork. Home Screen widgets use a shared on-device container accessible to Ateru and its widget extension for timeline data and cover images.

Deleting the app may remove its local data. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing devices. Device backups and diagnostics provided by Apple follow your operating-system settings and Apple's policies.

## Catalog requests

Search, browsing, imports and metadata refreshes contact public catalog services. AniList is the primary provider. Current TestFlight builds can also attempt requests to Kitsu and the retired public Jikan endpoint. MyAnimeList hosts artwork that may be loaded from catalog results or saved entries. If you enable advanced Detail information, the app also asks AniList for the selected studios, characters/voice cast, staff, tags or trailer metadata. Opening a trailer contacts YouTube through the in-app browser. Cover images may come from the associated AniList, Kitsu or MyAnimeList image servers.

Requests can contain search text, filters and catalog identifiers for series you look up or refresh. The provider receives ordinary network information, including your IP address. Requested identifiers may reflect series in your library. Ateru does not upload your complete archive, progress, scores, notes, local watch-event identifiers or import files to these providers, and does not add an advertising or tracking identifier.

Ateru is independent. The catalog, artwork and video services it contacts handle requests under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Choices you control

Import files are processed on your device; catalog identifiers may be looked up to resolve metadata. Export and recap sharing use the system share sheet. You choose where those files or images go, and the destination service applies its own policies.

You can inspect, export or clear your archive in Settings → Data. Clearing your library removes its records on this device. Exported files and device backups are managed separately. Ateru has no account or developer-operated copy of your archive. Optional themes and alternate icons use Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and offer-code redemption; Ateru checks verified on-device StoreKit entitlements to enable customization and restore access. Ateru does not receive your payment-card details or send purchase data to an Ateru server. Tips are disabled.

## Motion

The Search dice picks a recommendation without a motion gesture. Motion-driven artwork respects the app and system motion preferences.

## Contact and changes

If you email support, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request. Please omit private notes, backups and other details that are unnecessary. Contact [rwetz00@gmail.com](mailto:rwetz00@gmail.com) for privacy questions or to request deletion of information you sent us. Updated policies will be posted with a new effective date. The app is not designed to collect information from children or to provide a children's account service.
