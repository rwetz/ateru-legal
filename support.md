# Ateru Support

For help with Ateru, email [rwetz00@gmail.com](mailto:rwetz00@gmail.com).

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; remove private notes or other personal information first.

Your library is stored on your device. Before reinstalling or changing devices, export a backup from Settings → Data. Deleting the app can remove its local library. You do not need an AniList account to use Ateru; catalog search needs an internet connection.

If a widget appears stale, open Ateru to refresh its saved timeline. If it still fails, include the widget size and your Home Screen appearance (full color, tinted or clear) in your report.
